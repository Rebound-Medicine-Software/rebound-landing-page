Who I Am
Joshua
Richards-Fisher
Graduate Sports Therapist · S&C Coach · Amateur MMA Athlete

I work at the intersection of clinical sports therapy and athletic performance, helping people recover from injury, build physical resilience, and compete at their best. My background spans hands-on clinical work, S&C programming, and sports technology, with a focus on force plate biomechanics and performance data.

I've been competing in MMA at the –70kg amateur level since 2020. That experience shapes everything I do: I know what it takes to train hard, manage your body through a camp, and perform when it matters. That's what I bring to every client relationship.

What You Get

Services

Sports Therapy

Assessment, diagnosis, and treatment of sport and exercise-related injuries. Soft tissue therapy, joint mobilisation, taping, and rehab programming tailored to your sport.

Rehabilitation

Structured return-to-sport and return-to-training protocols. Progressive programming bridging the gap between clinical recovery and full competitive performance.

S&C Coaching

Individualised strength & conditioning programmes for athletes at all levels. Periodised training built around your sport's demands, your body's capacity, and your competitive goals.

Force Plate Analysis

Objective measurement of ground reaction forces, jump mechanics, asymmetries, and power output. Data-driven insights to optimise performance and reduce injury risk.

Concussion Management

Structured assessment and return-to-play protocols for concussion and head injuries. Evidence-based management for combat sport athletes and contact sport players.

Athlete Performance Plans

End-to-end performance planning for competitive athletes, from assessment to programming to monitoring, built to peak on fight night, matchday, or tournament.

What You Get

Services

Injury Assessment & Diagnosis

Hands-on assessment, diagnosis, and pain management for sport, exercise, and everyday injuries, whoever you are and however it happened. Soft tissue therapy, joint mobilisation, taping, and rehab programming tailored to you.

Rehabilitation

Structured return-to-sport, return-to-work, and return-to-everyday-life protocols. Progressive programming bridging the gap between clinical recovery and feeling fully back to yourself, whatever that looks like for you.

S&C Coaching

Individualised strength & conditioning programmes for athletes at all levels. Periodised training built around your sport's demands, your body's capacity, and your competitive goals.

Force Plate Analysis

Objective measurement of ground reaction forces, jump mechanics, asymmetries, and power output. Data-driven insights to optimise performance and reduce injury risk.

Concussion Management

Structured assessment and return-to-play protocols for concussion and head injuries, whether from sport, a fall, or an accident. Evidence-based management for anyone who's taken a knock to the head.

Athlete Performance Plans

End-to-end performance planning for competitive athletes, from assessment to programming to monitoring, built to peak on fight night, matchday, or tournament.
